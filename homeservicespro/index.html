Got Questions? We Have Honest Answers.

Everything homeowners ask before hiring us — answered clearly, with no jargon.

Getting Started
How do I get a quote?
Fill out our quick online form or call us directly. We'll schedule a free on-site assessment and provide a written estimate — usually within 24 hours of your request.
Is the estimate really free?
Yes, completely. We assess your project, explain what's needed, and give you a written quote at no cost. You decide whether to proceed.
How soon can you start?
For most projects, we can schedule within 3-7 business days. Emergency services are available same-day, often within hours.
Pricing & Payment
Will the price change after I approve the quote?
Only if the scope of work changes — and only with your written approval. We don't add costs mid-project without your knowledge.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, bank transfer, and check. For larger projects, we offer staged payment schedules tied to project milestones.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. We offer 0% financing for qualified homeowners on projects over $3,000. Ask us for details during your consultation.
Licensing & Insurance
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We hold a current state General Contractor License, carry $2M in general liability insurance, and all technicians are covered under our workers' compensation policy.
Will you pull permits for my project?
Absolutely. We handle all permit applications for projects that require them — it's part of the job, not an extra.
Warranties & Guarantees
What warranty do you offer on your work?
All labor is covered by our 12-month workmanship warranty. If anything we installed or repaired fails due to our workmanship, we fix it free of charge.
What if I'm not satisfied with the finished work?
We don't consider a job done until you're fully satisfied. We'll revisit and correct anything that doesn't meet your expectations at no additional cost.
